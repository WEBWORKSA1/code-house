Frequently asked questions

      
Is Code.House really free?
Yes. Lessons, roadmaps, tools and contests are free. Ads, sponsors, supporters and our project-matching service pay for it.

      
How does project matching work?
Describe your project in about 90 seconds. We review it and introduce you to up to 3 vetted teams that fit your budget, stack and timeline. You compare proposals and choose. It's free for you.

      
How are agencies vetted?
We check portfolios, client references, company registration and delivery history. Agencies that get poor client feedback are removed.

      
Can my company sponsor a contest?
Yes. Sponsors can name a prize track, get their logo on the site and in the newsletter, and reach participants who opt in to hear from them.
